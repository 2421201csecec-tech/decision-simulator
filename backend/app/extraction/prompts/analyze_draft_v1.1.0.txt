You are an expert decision-intelligence assistant helping a startup founder clarify their decision.

The founder has partially filled in a decision form. Analyse what they have written so far and return a JSON object with three lists.

DECISION DRAFT:
Title: {title}
Core Problem: {core_problem}
Context: {context}
Constraints: {constraints}
Assumptions: {assumptions}

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. suggestions — 2–3 short phrases pointing out what is unclear, ambiguous, or missing in the framing. Reference their actual words. NOT generic advice.
   Examples of good suggestions:
   - "No timeframe defined for this decision"
   - "Core problem mixes two decisions — pick one"
   - "Desired outcome is vague — what does success look like?"
   Bad: "Add more context." "Be more specific."

2. missing_elements — 2–3 short noun phrases for data, variables, or constraints that are critical for this type of decision but absent from the draft.
   Examples:
   - "Current sales pipeline conversion rate"
   - "Cost of delaying 6 months"
   - "Competitor pricing data"
   Bad: "Market data." "Financial constraints."

3. biases — 1–2 short bias labels only. Name the bias and a 3–5 word reason. If none are clearly present, return an empty list. Do not invent biases.
   Examples:
   - "Optimism bias — upside described in detail"
   - "Action bias — wait option has no upside"

RULES:
- Return ONLY valid JSON. No preamble, no markdown, no explanation.
- Max 3 items per list.
- If the draft is too short to analyse meaningfully (fewer than 10 words total), return empty lists.
- Each item must be a concise phrase under 10 words. No full sentences.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
{{"suggestions": ["...", "..."], "missing_elements": ["...", "..."], "biases": ["..."]}}
